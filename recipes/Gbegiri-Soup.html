Gbegiri Soup


Description

Gbegiri is a savory, nutrient-rich soup made from peeled brown or black-eyed beans. The beans are cooked until soft and then mashed or sieved to create a silky, cream-like consistency. Infused with palm oil, smoked fish, and local spices, it offers a unique earthy flavor that is both comforting and deeply traditional.

Ingredients

  • 1 cups Beans (white or brown)
  • Potash
  • Onions (2 balls)
  • Pepper
  • Palm oil
  • Seasoning (knorr cube, salt etc)
  • 900g fish/meat - optional

Steps

  1. Soak the beans and peel off the skins completely.
  2. Boil the peeled beans in water until they are very soft and mushy.
  3. Blend or mash the cooked beans and pass them through a sieve for a smooth texture.
  4. Pour the bean base into a pot and add palm oil, crayfish, and locust beans.
  5. Add salt, seasoning cubes, and pepper, then simmer on low heat for 5–10 minutes.
