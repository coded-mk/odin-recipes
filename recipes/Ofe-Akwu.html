Ofe Akwu


Description

Ofe Akwu (Banga Soup) is a flavorful palm fruit soup native to the Igbo people of Southeast Nigeria. Unlike the Delta version, the Igbo version is specifically seasoned with "Ogiri" (fermented oil seeds) and fresh scent leaves, giving it a distinct, aromatic punch that makes it the ultimate companion for white rice.

Ingredients

  • Palm fruit extract (concentrate)
  • Beef, shaki, and dry fish
  • Scent leaves (Nchanwu) and Ugu
  • Ground crayfish and Ogiri Okpei
  • Fresh pepper and onions

Steps

  1. Extract the oil from boiled palm fruits by mashing and straining with warm water.
  2. Boil the meat and dry fish with onions and seasoning until tender.
  3. Add the palm fruit extract to the meat stock and cook until it starts to thicken.
  4. Stir in the crayfish, pepper, and Ogiri, allowing the flavors to meld.
  5. Add the chopped scent leaves and Ugu, simmer for 2 minutes, and serve.
