Miyan Kuka


Description

Miyan Kuka is perhaps the most famous soup from Northern Nigeria. It is made from powdered baobab leaves, which gives it a unique dark green color and a slightly slippery texture similar to Okra soup. It is highly nutritious and a beloved staple in Hausa households.

Ingredients

  • Kuka powder (dried, ground baobab leaves)
  • Dried fish or smoked meat
  • Yaji (suya spice) or ginger powder
  • Dawa-dawa (fermented locust beans)
  • Palm oil or vegetable oil

Steps

  1. Boil your meat and dried fish with spices to create a rich, savory broth.
  2. Add the dawa-dawa and a little palm oil to the boiling stock.
  3. Reduce the heat and slowly whisk in the Kuka powder to avoid lumps.
  4. Keep stirring until the soup becomes smooth and slightly thickened.
  5. Simmer on low heat for 5 minutes and serve hot with Tuwo.
