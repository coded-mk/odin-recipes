Edikang Ikong


Description

Hailing from the Cross River and Akwa Ibom states, Edikang Ikong is a world-renowned "vegetable soup" known for its richness. It is traditionally prepared with a massive amount of Waterleaf and Ugu, making it a nutrient-dense meal that was once considered a luxury for royalty.

Ingredients

  • Fresh Waterleaf and Ugu (Fluted Pumpkin leaves)
  • Assorted meat, Ponmo, and Periwinkles
  • Smoked fish and Stockfish
  • Plenty of ground crayfish and palm oil
  • Fresh yellow pepper (Cameroon pepper)

Steps

  1. Steam the meat, stockfish, and periwinkles with minimal water until very soft.
  2. Add a generous amount of palm oil, crayfish, and pepper to the pot.
  3. Stir in the chopped waterleaf first and let it cook down for about 3 minutes.
  4. Add the chopped Ugu leaves and stir thoroughly to combine.
  5. Let it simmer for only 2 more minutes to keep the vegetables fresh and vibrant.
